Privacy Policy

How ParkMemo handles your information, and the choices you have.

Parking records stay on your device. Mapping, email, and website services are explained separately below.

01. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the ParkMemo iPhone app, its companion widgets, and this support website. The ParkMemo developer is responsible for the app processing and user support described here. For privacy questions or requests, contact dev.mintolaw@gmail.com.

ParkMemo helps you remember your parking location, floor, time, and photos. No account is required. The current app does not integrate advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs and does not automatically upload your parking records, photos, or sensor records to a developer-operated server.

02. Data processed by the app and its purposes

  • Location and saved places: With your permission, the app processes precise coordinates, location timestamps and accuracy, parking places, and the names and boundaries of areas you save. These support parking records, address display, and parking-area monitoring. If you allow background location, the app may receive location or region events while it is not in the foreground to support automatic parking records and floor-reference measurements.
  • Pressure, relative altitude, and floor corrections: The app reads air pressure and relative altitude changes from system sensors and combines them with parking areas, events, and your floor corrections to estimate and calibrate floors on your device. This learning takes place on the device.
  • Trip and parking records: The app processes vehicle connection or disconnection events, parking times, trip state, floors, parking duration, and associated location and sensor measurements. Vehicle events come from automations you configure in Apple Shortcuts; ParkMemo does not scan nearby Bluetooth devices for this feature.
  • Parking photos: You may choose to take a photo or select one through the system photo picker. The app processes only the captured or selected image and stores it locally with your parking record; it does not request browsing access to your entire photo library.
  • Preferences and local diagnostics: The app stores settings such as language, floor options, notification preferences, and onboarding progress. It also keeps a limited local diagnostic history for operation and troubleshooting. Entries may include event times, locations, regions, pressure, permission states, and errors. The current limit is the latest 5,000 entries; they are not automatically sent to the developer.

03. Permissions, widgets, and notifications

Location permission supports parking locations and region monitoring. Motion & Fitness permission supports pressure and relative-altitude readings. Camera permission is used when you choose to photograph your parking spot. Notification permission allows automatic parking confirmations. You can decline these permissions or revoke them in iPhone Settings; related features may then be limited.

The app and its companion widgets share recent parking information through local shared storage, not with other developers. Widgets and notifications may show parking information on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You can remove widgets, turn off notifications, or adjust notification previews. Parking notifications are local and do not require a developer-operated push server.

04. When information may leave your device

  • Apple Maps and system services: Map display, place search, address lookup, and navigation use Apple mapping and location services. Relevant coordinates, search terms, map areas, or navigation destinations may be processed by Apple and its applicable providers, depending on the feature, region, and your settings. See Apple Maps & Privacy and the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Device backups: ParkMemo has no developer-operated cloud-sync service. App data may be included in system backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings. You and the relevant system service control those backups.
  • Support you request: If you email the developer, we receive your sender address, message, and any screenshots or files you choose to attach. We use them to reply, troubleshoot, and address privacy requests. The support mailbox uses Gmail, and relevant email providers process those messages. See the Google Privacy Policy. Avoid including private information unrelated to your request.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. We do not disclose support information to unrelated third parties except to provide requested support, for sharing you explicitly choose, or as required by applicable law.

05. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Parking history, photos, calibration data, and preferences generally remain on your device for future use. Older local diagnostic entries are replaced as new entries arrive. You can manage saved parking areas in the app or use Delete App in iPhone Settings to remove the app and its local data. Offloading the app while keeping documents and data is not the same as deleting it. System backups and copies you separately save or share must be managed separately.

To stop background location processing, revoke ParkMemo’s location permission in system settings. You can also disable or delete its automations in Shortcuts and revoke camera, Motion & Fitness, or notification permissions in system settings.

We retain support emails only for the time needed to handle your request and necessary follow-up, or to retain records required by applicable law. You can email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent us, and we will handle your request under applicable requirements. The developer cannot remotely access or delete parking data stored only on your device.

06. This website, processing locations, and security

This website uses no advertising, visitor analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or online feedback form. Language selection is carried in the URL. Email links open your email app; you decide whether to send a message.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting, mapping, backup, and email providers may process data outside your country or region, depending on the service and its policies.

Local app data benefits from iOS app isolation and system security protections, and this website uses HTTPS. Protect access to your device, backups, and email account. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

07. Minors and policy updates

ParkMemo is not specifically designed for children. If you are a minor, seek a parent or guardian’s help with permissions and support requests where needed. Contact the developer if you believe a minor sent us personal information that should be deleted.

When app features or data practices change, we will update this page and its revision date. Changes requiring additional notice or permission will be handled under applicable requirements. You can contact us at any time with questions about the current policy.
